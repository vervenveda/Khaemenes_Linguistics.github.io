# Khaemenes Academy · French 201 · Unit 01 Foundation

Status: **instructional foundation / pilot**. This is a new learner-facing page, not a replacement for French 101/102 or an independently issued formal transcript.

## Repository grounding
- `Halls/index.html` describes *French 201 · Intermediate College I*: education/work, media, environment, arts/identity; cohesive paragraphs, relative clauses, future/conditional and subjunctive, intermediate reading and conversation.
- `data/course-topology.json` and `docs/TOPOLOGY_ARCHITECTURE.md` assign formal Five-Language course authority to `Halls/index.html`. The new `apps/French_201_index.html` is a **learning experience and evidence surface**.
- `docs/FRENCH_101_DAILY_LESSON_STANDARD.md` supplies the general ten-stage pedagogy and privacy/print/reviewer safeguards. French 201 raises the complexity rather than recycling beginner lessons.

## Course structure (foundation only)
- Unit 01 · Études, choix et avenir · Lessons 01–06 · **implemented learning pilots**.
- Unit 02 · Media, environment and evidence · Lessons 07–12 · planned.
- Unit 03 · Arts, identity and perspectives · Lessons 13–18 · planned.
- Suggested prerequisite: French 102 competencies, with a non-punitive readiness review rather than an invented automatic transcript transfer.

## Unit 01 lessons
1. Passer de la phrase au paragraphe — claims, reasons, contrasts, paragraph cohesion.
2. Parler de ses projets au futur — futur simple, futur proche and study plans.
3. Exprimer des possibilités et des conseils — polite conditional, possible choices, advice.
4. Relier ses idées avec qui, que et où — basic relative clauses for precision.
5. Lire et comparer des points de vue — intermediate source comprehension and comparison.
6. Mission : construire son parcours — integrated, evidence-based study pathway project.

Each lesson includes objectives, a teacher-authored French model, an original intermediate-level passage, interpretive questions, guided practice, a written production prompt, correction criteria and a locally saved portfolio. Optional French speech synthesis is **rehearsal, not an assessed listening result**.

Questions must be independently authored, validated, balanced across actual lesson skills, and unique within an attempt. Monte Carlo retakes require new balanced draws, saved attempts and learner-specific recovery. **None of these formal exams are represented as implemented in the Unit 01 foundation.** Do not activate fake pass buttons or fabricate grades while the banks are being written.

## Privacy and progress
- Save local learner writing and reflection only under the namespaced `khae.fr201.unit01.evidence.v1` keys, separate from French 101 and formal V4 records.
- The learner-label selector is convenience, **not authentication or cross-device synchronization**. Guest work is shared by anyone using the same browser.
- Portable evidence export is present; future restore must validate identity, schema and versions, protect overwritten evidence and offer recovery.
- Progress counters represent visiting and saved writing only; neither is equivalent to 80% graded mastery.
- The new French 201 page should **not be promoted as a formally mastered pathway** until academic authority integration and assessment gates are tested.
