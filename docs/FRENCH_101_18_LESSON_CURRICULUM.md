# French 101 · Canonical 18-Lesson Curriculum Contract

**Course:** Khaemenes Academy French 101  
**Canonical curriculum ID:** `KH-LANG-FR-101-2026`  
**Duration:** 18 flexible instructional weeks  
**Mastery threshold:** 80%

## Preservation rule

This contract does not renumber or delete the historical 12-level French 101 course, its progress, final results or certificates. The newer curriculum is developed lesson-by-lesson. When a newer lesson replaces an older daily topic, the older instruction and learner evidence remain available as optional enrichment/archive material.

## Six-unit instructional spine

| Unit | Lessons | Focus |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 · Foundations of French | 01–03 | Alphabet; Numbers; Vowels & Subject Pronouns |
| 2 · First Communication & Structure | 04–06 | Greetings; First Sentences; Nouns/Articles/Gender |
| 3 · People & Description | 07–09 | Être/Avoir; Family/Relationships; Colors/Shapes/Descriptions |
| 4 · Everyday Life | 10–12 | Dates/Time; Food/Café; Home/School/Objects |
| 5 · Independent Interaction | 13–15 | Questions/Negation; Present Verbs/Routines; Directions/Transportation |
| 6 · Integrated French | 16–18 | Reading/Listening; Writing/Speaking; Integration/Final Preparation |

## Assessment repair

Daily practice remains formative. It never silently awards formal course mastery.

The modern course will use:

1. six thematic unit reviews;
2. a versioned cumulative checkpoint after Lesson 06;
3. a cumulative midterm after Lesson 12;
4. a comprehensive final after Lesson 18;
5. separately identified writing, speaking and independent listening evidence.

The existing 15-item Unit 01 checkpoint and 10-translation final remain preserved for historical compatibility. They are not deleted and previously earned results are never downgraded.

The future midterm/final require 80% on the objective knowledge component and explicit review of productive evidence. A no-audio text fallback changes what was measured and cannot be counted as independent listening.

## Reviewer roles

Review is intentionally accessible for a free online/homeschool platform. The reviewer may be a Parent, Teacher, Homeschool Parent/Teacher, Mentor, Self-Directed Adult or Other Local Reviewer. The record should identify the role and evidence reviewed, not require a privileged teacher account. Independent third-party attestation is required only when an assessment explicitly calls for it.

## Development status

Lessons 01–08 are the protected developed baseline. Lesson 09 is the next full-development target. Lessons 10–18 follow sequentially.

The older Lessons 09–15 phonology/spelling sequence is valuable material and should be retained as a Pronunciation, Orthography & Literacy enrichment archive rather than discarded or mislabeled as the new curriculum.
