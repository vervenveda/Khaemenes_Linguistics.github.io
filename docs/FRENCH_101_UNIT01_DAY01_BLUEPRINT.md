# French 101 · Unit 01 · Lesson 01 — Reference Blueprint

**Status:** Working instructional reference; not yet live-browser or educator certified.  
**Source:** `apps/French_101_index.html` on `main`.  
**Scope:** One day only. No retroactive learner-progress migration.

## Core objective
A learner can independently identify and sequence 26 French alphabet letters, distinguish letter names from sounds within words, observe and preserve accents, spell a name accurately, comprehend a short original French exchange and compose a new exchange.

## Learning evidence map

| Strand | Teaching | Guided evidence | Independent evidence | How it is reviewed |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Letter order | A–Z four groups | identify next/previous | write letters without model | correct sequence and self-correction |
| Letter names | French audio examples | repeat four chunks | spell two names aloud | actual observed audio performance; no automatic credit |
| Accents | é, è, ê, ë, ç with examples | recognize marks | copy and use accented names | spelling rubric and correction log |
| Meaning | Léa / école dialogue | identify greeting, name, school and spelling request | explain passage | meaning and comprehension review |
| Written production | model dialogue | guided name-spelling practice | write original two-person exchange | independent writing rubric |
| Transfer | compare letter name vs phoneme | worked examples | explain and fix novel errors | transfer items and correction log |

## Daily session order
1. Open **Lesson 01** from Level 1, confirm title and alphabet objective.
2. Read **Why spelling and sound differ**, then compare three worked examples.
3. Practice French alphabet letter names, ideally with French voice or educator; written fallback does not certify listening.
4. Identify accents and try the guided questions. First five fixed questions are **warm-up** only.
5. Read the original short passage, listen where available, explain the meaning.
6. Complete the **10-item balanced review**, drawn from 30 authored questions.
7. Write an original dialogue in the separately saved field.
8. Retrieve A–Z without looking, log errors, correct accents and explain one spelling decision.
9. Complete existing printable worksheet and 0–3 educator or self-review rubric.
10. Return to the level course journey. **Neither formative review nor games unlock the course.** The existing official Level 1 80% quiz and distinct Unit 01 checkpoint retain their own records and gates.

## Authored formative review
- 30 questions in five strands: 5 alphabet-order, 8 accent-recognition, 5 meaning, 5 usage and 7 transfer items.
- A round selects two questions in each of the five strands (10 total), with shuffled answer choices.
- Correct-answer explanations appear after a selection; questions cannot be scored twice in the same round.
- Review attempts are intentionally **not persisted** as mastery; students may retry without penalty.
- The former five-question check remains as a warm-up, not as a second mastery requirement.
- **Next refinement:** introduce independently evaluated written spelling and an unfamiliar listening item; do not mislabel text recognition as oral proficiency.
