## Required before any lesson quiz is released
1. At least 27 distinct, hand-reviewed questions per lesson, each with an accurate key and explanation; no formulaic filler.
2. Check spelling, grammar, register, Francophone variation, meaningful distractors, and alignment to actual teaching content.
3. At least two assessed domains, including interpretation and transfer whenever the lesson supports them.
4. Test 20 unique questions per draw, 16 correct to pass, failure and retake recovery, one-third bank reserve, and never claim zero repeated questions.
5. Keep lesson scores, written work, reviewed speaking, and independent listening evidence separate; browser audio is practice, not proficiency verification.
6. Browser/test-device review, accessibility, offline behavior, learner-record isolation, preservation snapshot and verified backup/export.
7. Unlock and formal authority integration remain **withheld** until these steps pass across all four grades.
