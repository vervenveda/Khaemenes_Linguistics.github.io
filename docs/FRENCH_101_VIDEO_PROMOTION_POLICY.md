# French 101 · Video Promotion and Review Policy

Status: **Required editorial gate**. No candidate is promoted merely because it is free, popular, or linked online. No external video's score counts toward student mastery.

## Five gates before an external video appears inside a daily lesson
1. **Rights and access** — Exact official source URL, creator, current free access, presence/absence of ads, link-vs-embed permissions and offline fallback. Never download or rehost without express authorization.
2. **Accuracy and alignment** — Native/qualified pronunciation, correct grammar, correct regional-language context, and exact fit to the daily lesson objective. Instructor reviews the entire video, not only title or description.
3. **Evidence of quality** — Record public rating, review count, review date and source **when available**, plus representative recent review themes and credible teacher/learner feedback. Distinguish app-wide ratings from *video-specific* ratings. No published video-specific ratings = `NOT AVAILABLE`, **never fabricate a score**. High popularity does not override pedagogical faults.
4. **Learner safety and accessibility** — Age-appropriate language, understandable pace, captions/transcript where available, accessible controls, no forced account, functional playback on tablet/mobile, no inappropriate links. Offer a complete text/original Academy alternative.
5. **Runtime verification and Academy sign-off** — Check URL resolves and video plays on expected devices. Review dated by Academy educator; assess original independent questions to accompany video; record pass/fail and next recheck date. No auto-promotions.

## Promotion rules
- Lesson resources only show `APPROVED LINK ONLY` or separately cleared embedded media, never candidates.
- A missing public rating is not automatic rejection if expert review and teaching tests are strong, but it must remain explicitly marked `NOT AVAILABLE`.
- Expired or relocated links revert to `NEEDS REVIEW`.
- Academic assessment must stand without the video; never make external playback or third-party account mandatory to earn mastery.
