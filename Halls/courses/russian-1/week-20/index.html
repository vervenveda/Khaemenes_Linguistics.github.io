Unit 03 · Practical Communication · Week 20

Russian I — Ordering and Preferences

Learn and use six expressions in a realistic, privacy-safe situation.

Vocabulary and patterns

ExpressionMeaning
Я хочуI want
мне нравитсяI like (literally 'is pleasing to me')
пожалуйстаplease
спасибоthank you
чайtea
сокjuice

Use teacher-verified models for pronunciation and contextual grammar. These beginner vocabulary examples do not establish mastery of inflection or dialect variation.

Four-skill guided learning

  1. Listening: identify new words in a qualified speaker's short demonstration. Without approved audio, record the listening task as pending.
  2. Speaking: act out a fictional situation and explain how you choose respectful expressions.
  3. Reading: read the six examples and match them to meanings with the translations covered.
  4. Writing: copy the expressions, then complete this independent application: Create a polite café dialogue with attention to grammatical context.

Applied performance task

Create a polite café dialogue with attention to grammatical context. Prepare a short written exchange or labelled diagram with at least three target-language expressions. A teacher checks meaningful language use, script accuracy and cultural context, not only vocabulary recall.

Worked conversation and grammar in context

Model A: Я хочу чай. — I want tea.

Model B: Мне нравится чай.

Notice: Я хочу takes a requested object; мне нравится follows a different sentence structure for expressing preference.

Guided transformation: Explain the difference between a request and a preference, using fictional drinks.

Misconception check: Explain how context or grammatical form changes the sentence. Check with a qualified instructor.

Independent evidence: Write a three-turn invented dialogue and demonstrate or annotate it. The teacher checks meaning and language accuracy separately from the quiz.

Week 20 formative quiz

Ten open-book vocabulary-recognition checks; 8/10 is a provisional readiness guide. Constructed responses, oral performance, and teacher feedback remain necessary.

Teacher notes

Five adaptable sessions of 30–45 minutes. Use explicit demonstration, feedback, pair practice, meaning checks, and supported independent work. Provide accommodations and use fictional names/places rather than requiring private details. No external services or automatic learner-record updates.
