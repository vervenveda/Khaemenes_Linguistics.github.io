Khaemenes Academy · Weeks 09–15

Russian I — Applied Practice Studio

Seven constructed-response assignments to deepen vocabulary-only quizzes

Purpose

These weekly applications supplement, rather than replace, the existing ten-question formative quizzes. Complete short reading, writing, or oral demonstrations for a teacher. Type here or print and write by hand; responses are not saved and are not submitted anywhere automatically.

For listening and speaking evidence, use a qualified speaker or verified recording. Invented characters are encouraged. Teachers may provide accessible alternative response formats.

Week 09 · Numbers / time

Constructed response: Identify один, три, пять; create a beginner-friendly timetable entry.

Teacher observation: Count accurately; avoid unintroduced case constructions.

Week 10 · Days / schedules

Constructed response: Make a fictional weekday schedule using понедельник and сегодня.

Teacher observation: Distinguish time reference from grammar of dates.

Week 11 · Classroom expressions

Constructed response: Demonstrate the meaning of Читайте! and Пишите! in a partner task.

Teacher observation: Notice polite/plural imperative forms.

Week 12 · School routines

Constructed response: Write three sentences using Я читаю., Я пишу., and Я учусь.

Teacher observation: Check first-person verb forms and meaning.

Week 13 · Family / descriptions

Constructed response: Introduce invented characters using брат and сестра.

Teacher observation: Avoid real family disclosure requirements.

Week 14 · Adjective agreement

Constructed response: Contrast новый, новая, новое with appropriate nouns.

Teacher observation: Check nominative singular gender agreement.

Week 15 · Possessives

Constructed response: Complete contextual phrases using мой, моя, моё.

Teacher observation: Determine agreement with the possessed noun.

Teacher evaluation guide

For each task, rate accuracy of meaning (0–2), language form (0–2), and communicative clarity (0–2), maximum 6. Record feedback separately using an approved student record. For learners needing accommodations, assess the intended language skill rather than handwriting motor control.

Interpretation: 6 exemplary, 5 proficient, 4 developing, 0–3 reteach. These teacher ratings do not automatically unlock progression or count as final exam scores.
