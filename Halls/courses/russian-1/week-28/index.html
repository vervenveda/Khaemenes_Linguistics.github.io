Unit 04 · Week 28

Russian I — Weather and seasons

Read, interpret, discuss and write expressions for everyday communication.

Vocabulary and language patterns

ExpressionMeaning
погодаweather
солнцеsun
дождьrain
зимаwinter
летоsummer

Sample expressions are introductory: grammar, pronunciation and regional forms require qualified review in context.

Guided four-skill lesson

  1. Listening: identify the expressions when spoken by a qualified teacher or verified recording; otherwise mark this step pending.
  2. Speaking: practice a short fictional exchange and compare the expressions with earlier lessons.
  3. Reading: cover the English meanings and identify the expressions from the target script.
  4. Writing: copy five expressions and create a four-line fictional mini-dialogue using at least three accurately.

Independent application and reflection

Prepare a short scenario relevant to weather and seasons. Add a context note about language register or cultural differences, and describe one spelling or grammar challenge. Teacher reviews use of meaningful sentences rather than scoring vocabulary recognition alone.

Students may use fictional identities and circumstances; personal information must never be required.

Worked sentences, grammar, and independent transfer

Read and interpret: Зимой часто холодно. — In winter it is often cold.

Compare: Летом бывает жарко.

What changes and why: In summer it can be hot.

Guided transformation: Impersonal weather expressions such as холодно and жарко do not require a personal subject; avoid universalizing climate patterns.

Misconception check: explain a possible wrong translation or inflection and how the example avoids it. Verify responses with a qualified instructor.

Portfolio evidence: preserve one independently written example, annotate its meaning and perform or interpret it in a teacher-supported conversation. Use fictional identities, and do not publish student records.

Week 28 formative knowledge quiz

Ten open-book vocabulary recognition questions. The 80% threshold is provisional and does not replace speaking, listening and writing evidence.

Teacher notes and accessibility

Five adaptable sessions of 30–45 minutes: model, compare, rehearse, write, correct, reflect. Provide larger text, keyboard access, assistive writing, alternate speech assessment and ample retry time. No third-party libraries or remote record submission.
