Year I · Week 32

Arabic I — Cumulative Final

Instructor assessment blueprint — not a finished secure examination

Coverage: Weeks 01–32, with 20 points each for reading, listening, speaking and writing (80 total).

A. Reading · 20 points

Teacher provides an unseen, beginner-level passage about a fictional learner's routine, weather, travel or shopping. Ask four comprehension questions worth five points each; assess meaning and evidence.

B. Listening · 20 points

Teacher prepares an unseen, verified dialogue with a greeting, two exchanges about schedules or preferences, and a direction or place description. Present twice; score five comprehension questions worth four points each.

C. Speaking · 20 points

Hold a 90–120 second fictional role-play covering introductions, at least two questions, daily activities and a practical request. Rubric: intelligibility, vocabulary, grammar and interaction, five points each.

D. Writing · 20 points

Write a 6–8 sentence message about a fictional person's week, preferences and location. Rubric: task completion, vocabulary, grammar, and script/spelling, five points each.

Provisional readiness benchmark: 64/80 overall (80%) subject to teacher review in each skill. Provide accommodations and permitted alternative modalities, practice feedback and reassessment.

Before administration: a qualified teacher must create and review unseen prompts, listening material, model responses, and grading keys. Keep secure exam content outside the public repository. This public blueprint cannot itself determine credit or award certificates.

Student privacy: fictional identities; do not require legal names or private personal details. No third-party libraries or automated record storage.
