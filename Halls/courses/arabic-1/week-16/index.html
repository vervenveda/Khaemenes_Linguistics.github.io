Unit 02 · Week 16 · Instructor-administered assessment

Arabic I — Midterm

Weeks 01–16 cumulative · closed-book administration recommended · 80-point analytic rubric

Assessment blueprint and printable response worksheet. The listening and speaking sections need a qualified teacher; this page does not claim an automatically graded exam.

Part A — Reading comprehension · 20 points

Read the short model text without help. In an official exam, the instructor should provide a parallel unseen passage of comparable difficulty.

مَرْحَبًا! اسْمِي لَيْلَى. أَنَا طَالِبَةٌ. أَدْرُسُ فِي مَدْرَسَةٍ. هَذَا كِتَابِي.

  1. Identify the speaker's name.
  2. Explain the school or learning activity.
  3. Identify the possession expression.
  4. Translate the short passage into clear English.

Scoring: four tasks × 5 points; judge meaning, not a word-for-word English calque.

Part B — Listening · 20 points

Teacher reads a separate, unseen adapted dialogue twice at natural but beginner-accessible speed. It must include a greeting, introduction, question, activity, and a schedule or number. Ask five listening comprehension questions, worth four points each. Do not use unreviewed generated speech.

Teacher prep: make the dialogue and answer key before administering the exam; this framework does not provide the unseen recording or keys.

Part C — Speaking · 20 points

Role-play a 60–90 second conversation with a teacher or approved partner. Include a greeting, fictional name, two questions and answers, and a polite closing. Score clarity/intelligibility (5), meaningful vocabulary (5), structures (5), and interaction (5). Accessible alternative responses may be approved.

Part D — Writing · 20 points

Compose five short MSA statements about a fictional learner using a greeting, name, school, noun and one possessive suffix.

Score message completeness (5), intelligibility (5), vocabulary (5), and script/grammatical control (5).

Midterm readiness and administration safeguards

Complete Unit 02 constructed-response practice (Weeks 09–15) before the midterm. This practice does not replace an unseen test.

Before use: the examiner must supply a separate unseen reading passage, a distinct listening script or human-recorded audio with five checked questions, approved keys, and a spoken performance prompt. Store secure test material outside the public repository. A second reviewer should check wording, cultural representativeness, difficulty and accommodations.

Fair scoring: the 80-point total is a framework; 64/80 is a provisional threshold. Review performance separately across reading, listening, speaking, and writing, offer feedback and reassessment, and never infer oral proficiency from the vocabulary quizzes alone.

Coverage, scoring, and teacher notes

Script joining, short vowels, greetings, simple sentences, numbers, classroom language, family, adjectives and possession.
