Unit 03 · Practical Communication · Week 24

Arabic I — Transport and Place Descriptions

Learn and use six expressions in a realistic, privacy-safe situation.

Vocabulary and patterns

ExpressionMeaning
حَافِلَةbus
قِطَارtrain
سَيَّارَةcar
مَحَطَّةstation
مَدِينَةcity
كَبِيرَةbig (feminine)

Use teacher-verified models for pronunciation and contextual grammar. These beginner vocabulary examples do not establish mastery of inflection or dialect variation.

Four-skill guided learning

  1. Listening: identify new words in a qualified speaker's short demonstration. Without approved audio, record the listening task as pending.
  2. Speaking: act out a fictional situation and explain how you choose respectful expressions.
  3. Reading: read the six examples and match them to meanings with the translations covered.
  4. Writing: copy the expressions, then complete this independent application: Produce a short accessible transit description with a labeled map.

Applied performance task

Produce a short accessible transit description with a labeled map. Prepare a short written exchange or labelled diagram with at least three target-language expressions. A teacher checks meaningful language use, script accuracy and cultural context, not only vocabulary recall.

Worked conversation and grammar in context

Model A: أَنَا فِي الْمَدِينَةِ. — I am in the city.

Model B: الْمَحَطَّةُ قَرِيبَةٌ.

Notice: The station is near.

Guided transformation: Location statements and feminine adjective agreement can describe travel landmarks.

Misconception check: Identify what would change if the setting, person, or grammatical form changed. Explain your reasoning and have a qualified instructor review the result.

Independent evidence: Write a three-turn fictional dialogue and perform or annotate it with a partner. Teacher checks accuracy, cultural register, and comprehension separately from the quiz.

Week 24 formative quiz

Ten open-book vocabulary-recognition checks; 8/10 is a provisional readiness guide. Constructed responses, oral performance, and teacher feedback remain necessary.

Teacher notes

Five adaptable sessions of 30–45 minutes. Use explicit demonstration, feedback, pair practice, meaning checks, and supported independent work. Provide accommodations and use fictional names/places rather than requiring private details. No external services or automatic learner-record updates.
