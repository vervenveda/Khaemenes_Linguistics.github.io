Arabic I — Week 9

Numbers and Time

Objectives: recognize six new expressions, interpret meaning, construct short statements, and build listening, speaking, reading and writing evidence.

1. Vocabulary and language focus

Numbers one to five and the first clock-time expression; formal numeral agreement is a later topic.

Arabic expressionIntroductory meaning
وَاحِدone
اِثْنَانtwo
ثَلَاثَةthree
أَرْبَعَةfour
خَمْسَةfive
السَّاعَةُ الْوَاحِدَةُone o'clock

2. Worked example and guided activities

Model A: وَاحِد — one.

Model B: اِثْنَان — two.

  1. Listen: have a qualified speaker or teacher model the six expressions; compare their sounds. If audio is unavailable, record the task as pending.
  2. Speak: rehearse all six expressions in pairs, then explain which form is appropriate in the situation.
  3. Read: cover the English column and match all six terms to their meanings. Ask a peer to verify.
  4. Write: copy each term accurately, then produce three short context-appropriate sentences or labeled examples using fictional people and situations.

Reflection: choose two expressions, identify a likely misconception, and explain how context helps clarify meaning.

3. Independent task

Make a small bilingual learning card or two-person mini-dialogue using three new expressions plus one greeting from Week 05. Use invented names and situations, not personal identifying information.

Teacher checkpoint: review correct script, comprehension, context and pronunciation separately. A ten-question vocabulary check cannot prove conversational proficiency.

4. Weekly quiz 09

Ten open-book recognition questions, 80% formative readiness guideline. To receive full week mastery credit, complete the speaking, writing and comprehension tasks with teacher feedback.

5. Teacher notes

Suggested pacing: five 30–45 minute sessions. Model, scaffold, retrieve, practice, and check understanding. Accommodation: larger script, keyboard access, typed responses, additional time, instructor-supported speech alternatives. Privacy: no personal data transmission, no third-party runtime or paid features.
