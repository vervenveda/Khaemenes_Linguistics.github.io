Khaemenes Academy · Weeks 09–15

Arabic I — Applied Practice Studio

Seven constructed-response assignments to deepen vocabulary-only quizzes

Purpose

These weekly applications supplement, rather than replace, the existing ten-question formative quizzes. Complete short reading, writing, or oral demonstrations for a teacher. Type here or print and write by hand; responses are not saved and are not submitted anywhere automatically.

For listening and speaking evidence, use a qualified speaker or verified recording. Invented characters are encouraged. Teachers may provide accessible alternative response formats.

Week 09 · Numbers / time

Constructed response: Write the words for one and three; create a simple one-o'clock statement.

Teacher observation: Distinguish number words from expressions of clock time.

Week 10 · Days / schedules

Constructed response: Write a two-line fictional weekly schedule using الْأَحَد and الْيَوْم.

Teacher observation: Read days in a calendar context without inventing date morphology.

Week 11 · Classroom commands

Constructed response: Explain the difference between اُكْتُبْ and اُكْتُبِي, then write a classroom instruction.

Teacher observation: Identify addressee agreement in formal commands.

Week 12 · School routines

Constructed response: Write three sentences with أَدْرُسُ, أَقْرَأُ, and أَكْتُبُ.

Teacher observation: Check first-person verb recognition and natural context.

Week 13 · Relationships

Constructed response: Describe a fictional family or chosen community using أُمّ and صَدِيقَة.

Teacher observation: Respect chosen and nontraditional family structures.

Week 14 · Agreement

Constructed response: Contrast كَبِير and كَبِيرَة in two noun phrases.

Teacher observation: Assess grammatical agreement rather than rote translation.

Week 15 · Possession

Constructed response: Interpret كِتَابِي, كِتَابُكَ, and كِتَابُكِ.

Teacher observation: Identify suffix reference and audience without identity assumptions.

Teacher evaluation guide

For each task, rate accuracy of meaning (0–2), language form (0–2), and communicative clarity (0–2), maximum 6. Record feedback separately using an approved student record. For learners needing accommodations, assess the intended language skill rather than handwriting motor control.

Interpretation: 6 exemplary, 5 proficient, 4 developing, 0–3 reteach. These teacher ratings do not automatically unlock progression or count as final exam scores.
