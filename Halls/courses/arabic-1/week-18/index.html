Unit 03 · Practical Communication · Week 18

Arabic I — Ordering Politely

Learn and use six expressions in a realistic, privacy-safe situation.

Vocabulary and patterns

ExpressionMeaning
مِنْ فَضْلِكَplease (to male)
مِنْ فَضْلِكِplease (to female)
أُرِيدُI want
شُكْرًاthank you
هَلْ عِنْدَكَdo you have (to male)
نَعَمْyes

Use teacher-verified models for pronunciation and contextual grammar. These beginner vocabulary examples do not establish mastery of inflection or dialect variation.

Four-skill guided learning

  1. Listening: identify new words in a qualified speaker's short demonstration. Without approved audio, record the listening task as pending.
  2. Speaking: act out a fictional situation and explain how you choose respectful expressions.
  3. Reading: read the six examples and match them to meanings with the translations covered.
  4. Writing: copy the expressions, then complete this independent application: Role-play a courteous market exchange, selecting appropriate forms of address.

Applied performance task

Role-play a courteous market exchange, selecting appropriate forms of address. Prepare a short written exchange or labelled diagram with at least three target-language expressions. A teacher checks meaningful language use, script accuracy and cultural context, not only vocabulary recall.

Polite market dialogue: full worked example

Teacher model: Read the full target-language example, first for meaning and then for form. Ask students to notice what changed.

الْبَائِعُ: مَرْحَبًا! — Seller: Hello!

الزَّبُونُ: أُرِيدُ مَاءً، مِنْ فَضْلِكَ. — Customer: I would like water, please (addressing a male).

الْبَائِعُ: نَعَمْ، تَفَضَّلْ. — Seller: Yes, here you are (addressing a male).

الزَّبُونُ: شُكْرًا. — Customer: Thank you.

Explain: Notice that أُرِيدُ introduces a request. The suffix on مِنْ فَضْلِكَ addresses a male; use مِنْ فَضْلِكِ when addressing a female. Spoken dialects may use other everyday polite forms.

Guided transfer: Write a two-turn exchange requesting bread instead of water. Explain what changes and why.

Misconception check: Ask the learner to explain the grammatical choice in their own words before independent writing. Do not mark pronunciation correct without qualified audio or teacher review.

Four-skill learning studio · Original practice passage

Read the target-language passage before consulting the English meaning reference. This is an original instructional exercise, not an authenticated recording or independently reviewed source.

السَّلَامُ عَلَيْكُمْ. مِنْ فَضْلِكَ، أُرِيدُ مَاءً. تَفَضَّلْ. شُكْرًا.

Meaning reference (check after reading): Peace be upon you. Please (to a male), I would like water. Here you are. Thank you.

Reading and reasoning: Identify the request, response and thanks; discuss register and context.

Listening: Conceal the printed text while a qualified instructor reads or plays an approved recording twice; summarize two details heard. There is no certified audio attached to this activity. Record listening as not assessed if none is available.

Speaking: Perform a comparable situation aloud with a teacher or partner; assess intelligibility, appropriate wording, response independence and cultural register. Do not use transliteration as evidence of pronunciation mastery.

Independent writing: Write a three-turn order and mark the polite expression.

Teacher rubric: Score reading 0–3, listening 0–3 only with valid audio evidence, speaking 0–3 only with an observed performance, and writing 0–3 with written revision feedback. Save a dated private portfolio record. A weekly multiple-choice quiz does not substitute for these four skills.

Connected reading · Market exchange

Original, fictional Modern Standard Arabic practice draft. Linguistic review of diacritics, grammatical endings and natural register is pending; do not use as an approved pronunciation model.

دَخَلَتْ سَلْمَى السُّوقَ فِي الصَّبَاحِ. قَالَتْ لِلْبَائِعِ: مَرْحَبًا، هَلْ عِنْدَكَ مَاءٌ؟ قَالَ: نَعَمْ، تَفَضَّلِي. قَالَتْ: أُرِيدُ مَاءً، مِنْ فَضْلِكَ. ثُمَّ قَالَتْ: شُكْرًا.

Read independently: Conceal the vocabulary glossary and identify the setting, actions and relationships. Annotate three language forms from the passage.

Comprehension and grammar: Where does Salma go? What does she request? Find one form addressed to a woman and one addressed to a man.

Independent writing: Replace the requested object with another lesson word; explain which forms of address change and why.

Listening boundary: Listening evidence is pending until a qualified instructor supplies and approves a recording or reads the passage live without exposing the text. No synthetic or unreviewed audio is counted as mastery.

Speaking and portfolio: Recount the fictional scene orally to an instructor, then privately save the written version and teacher feedback under Arabic I · Week 18. A quiz result alone does not certify speaking or writing proficiency.

Week 18 formative quiz

Ten open-book vocabulary-recognition checks; 8/10 is a provisional readiness guide. Constructed responses, oral performance, and teacher feedback remain necessary.

Teacher notes

Five adaptable sessions of 30–45 minutes. Use explicit demonstration, feedback, pair practice, meaning checks, and supported independent work. Provide accommodations and use fictional names/places rather than requiring private details. No external services or automatic learner-record updates.
