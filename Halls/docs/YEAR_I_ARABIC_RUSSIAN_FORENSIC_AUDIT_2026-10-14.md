# Arabic I and Russian I — Year I Forensic Audit · 2026-10-14

## Scope and method
Source-level review of published lesson patterns, Weeks 01–32, syllabus links, weekly assessment construction, and midterm/final blueprints. A committed script in this directory supports repeatable offline verification of all 64 lesson pages after cloning this repository. Live browser testing and qualified language-specialist review are **not** claimed.

## Findings and remediation priorities
1. **Quiz diversity — high priority.** Weeks 09–15, 17–24 and 25–31 have ten questions per week but typically only five or six vocabulary targets. Questions cycle through the same targets and test recognition rather than sentence-level understanding, listening, or productive use. The 80% threshold on such quizzes must not be treated as course mastery. Add varied grammar/comprehension items and independent performance rubrics before awarding formal credit.
2. **Navigation — high priority.** Standalone lesson files offer a return to Linguistics Hall, but some lack next/previous week, a course table of contents, and visible progress through the 32-week sequence. Implement a local course index and accessible week links.
3. **Assessment security — high priority.** Week 16 and Week 32 pages are public frameworks, not complete secure examinations. Unseen prompts, examiner-approved listening material, answer keys, accommodation and validity review are outstanding. Keep secure answers outside public GitHub.
4. **Listening and speaking — high priority.** Most lessons assign activities with no verified recorded audio. Retain teacher-mediated alternatives; never claim automatic pronunciation assessment.
5. **Language accuracy — review required.** Short glosses and isolated expressions do not capture all case inflections, stress, gender agreement, idiomatic conventions, dialect variation or phonetics. Have Arabic and Russian instructors review prompts and speaker-register examples before summative use.
6. **Offline and profiles — validation pending.** Individual lesson quiz scores are ephemeral, and student profile pinning does not prove grade persistence or cross-device sync. Test keyboard behavior, printing, offline opening and real student journeys in browsers.

## Exit criteria for academic readiness
- All 64 lesson pages have valid internal links and pass a browser smoke test (not only static inspection).
- Every weekly quiz uses distinct educational targets (recognition, forms, context, short response) and a reviewed answer key.
- Audio and teacher alternative provided for listening; oral rubric for speaking.
- Each course has secure instructor-controlled assessments and independently reviewed marking materials.
- Student progress recording/restore and course pinning are tested with multiple student profiles on different devices.
- External language and accessibility review signs off before credit claims.

**Status:** Development curriculum; source-level testing does not confer certification or credit.
