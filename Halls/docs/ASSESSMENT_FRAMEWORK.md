# ASSESSMENT_FRAMEWORK.md

## Why V4 changes assessment
The V3 Hall had 200 useful module-specific questions, but they assess language-learning knowledge and stewardship more than actual Spanish, French, German, Mandarin or Japanese performance.

V4 preserves those questions inside the Foundations Library but makes formal course completion performance-based.

## Six performance evidence channels
- Interpretive Listening
- Interpretive Reading
- Interpersonal Speaking
- Presentational Speaking
- Presentational Writing
- Mediation / Connections

## Course completion gate
A course can be recorded complete when:
- all thematic units are marked complete;
- at least 5 of 6 performance tasks are reviewed;
- at least 8 of 11 competencies are Secure;
- midterm is 80% or higher;
- final is 80% or higher;
- capstone reflection is at least 250 characters;
- portfolio / performance evidence is marked reviewed.

These are Khaemenes internal mastery rules, not an external proficiency rating.

## Midterm and final
The recommended format is an Integrated Performance Assessment:
1. interpret authentic/adapted input;
2. interact about it;
3. present or write a response using course-level language.
