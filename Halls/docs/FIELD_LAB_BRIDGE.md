# FIELD_LAB_BRIDGE.md

The Five-Language Academy is designed to supply curricular content and evidence goals to a future universal Field Lab Language Immersion Pack.

Possible scenarios:
- train station / transit disruption
- host-family arrival
- restaurant / food allergy communication
- school or university orientation
- market / shopping problem
- museum / cultural site
- doctor / pharmacy information boundary
- interview / workplace introduction
- travel lodging issue
- community event
- source-based discussion

The Hall should remain the curriculum authority.
Field Lab should become the experiential pressure-test layer.

A future pack should use the universal Engine → Hall Pack → Scenario architecture rather than hard-coding one language.
