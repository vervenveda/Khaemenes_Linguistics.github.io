# Arabic — High School I–IV · 32-Week Course Outlines

**Status:** Outline and assessment blueprint only; complete lessons, answer keys, exams, and credit certification are not yet published.

**Sequence:** Year I and Year II are the Academy's two-year expected world-language pathway; Years III and IV offer optional continuation. Students may enter by demonstrated proficiency rather than grade alone. The language selection and profile pin do not themselves confer credit.

**Approach:** Four 8-week units per level. Each week has listening, speaking, reading, and writing objectives, vocabulary, practice, and a **weekly formative quiz**. **Week 16:** cumulative midterm. **Week 32:** cumulative final. Quizzes in examination weeks are integrated into the cumulative assessment rather than duplicating a separate test. Student accessibility, dialect awareness, and respectful cultural treatment are required.

- [ ] Create and review 30 weekly quiz banks plus integrated Week 16 midterm and Week 32 final per level.
- [ ] Prepare teacher notes, rubrics, answer keys, differentiation, accommodations, and offline printable packets.
- [ ] Connect each course to the high-school language selector, course pins, and learner progress only after testing.
- [ ] Verify script direction, pronunciation, culturally sound examples, and language-specific handwriting guidance with qualified reviewers.
