# PROFICIENCY_AND_STANDARDS_FRAMEWORK.md

## ACTFL
The V4 Academy uses the ACTFL Proficiency Guidelines 2024 as a major U.S. proficiency reference. ACTFL describes proficiency across listening, speaking, reading and writing and uses the FACT criteria:
- Functions and tasks
- Accuracy
- Context and content
- Text type

The Academy also uses the World-Readiness Standards' five goal areas:
- Communication
- Cultures
- Connections
- Comparisons
- Communities

## CEFR
CEFR and the 2020 Companion Volume are used as reference frameworks for task-based progression, mediation, online interaction and plurilingual/pluricultural competence.

## No automatic ACTFL ↔ CEFR equivalency
The course cards may show both an ACTFL-oriented target and a CEFR reference band. These are **parallel curriculum references**, not an official one-to-one conversion.
