# Spanish · Español

Framework reference: Instituto Cervantes Plan Curricular · ACTFL · CEFR reference

## Middle School 1 · MS Spanish 1 · First Conversations

**Length:** 36 weeks  
**ACTFL-oriented performance target:** Novice Low → Novice Mid  
**CEFR reference band:** Pre-A1 / A1 reference

**Themes:** identity & greetings · school & classroom · family & people · numbers, time & calendar · food & preferences · places & simple directions

**Language forms:** sound-spelling, articles, gender/number, ser, estar, hay, tener, llamarse, gustar, present tense, question words

**Literacy:** phonetic decoding, short messages, labels, menus and beginner dialogues

**Culture/context:** greetings and address across communities; names; school life; foodways without reducing Spanish-speaking cultures to one country

**Performance goal:** introduce yourself, ask/answer familiar questions, read a short notice, write a 6–8 sentence profile

## Middle School 2 · MS Spanish 2 · Daily Life & Community

**Length:** 36 weeks  
**ACTFL-oriented performance target:** Novice Mid → Novice High  
**CEFR reference band:** A1 reference

**Themes:** daily routines · home & neighborhood · shopping · weather & clothing · leisure & sports · travel basics

**Language forms:** regular/irregular present, reflexives, stem-changing verbs, direct objects, ir + a, comparatives, demonstratives

**Literacy:** short personal narratives, schedules, advertisements, simple webpages

**Culture/context:** neighborhoods, markets, sports, youth media and regional vocabulary

**Performance goal:** sustain a short exchange, narrate a routine, compare two places, write a practical message

## Middle School 3 · MS Spanish 3 · Stories, Past & Future

**Length:** 36 weeks  
**ACTFL-oriented performance target:** Novice High  
**CEFR reference band:** A1 / A2 reference

**Themes:** childhood & memory · celebrations · health & wellbeing · transportation · nature & environment · future plans

**Language forms:** preterite foundations, imperfect introduction, commands, por/para introduction, present progressive, future with ir a

**Literacy:** graded stories, short biographies, cultural notes and simple informational texts

**Culture/context:** celebrations, environmental issues, public transportation and youth perspectives across regions

**Performance goal:** tell a simple past event, give advice/instructions, interpret a short story, present a future plan

## High School I · Spanish I · Novice-High Performance
