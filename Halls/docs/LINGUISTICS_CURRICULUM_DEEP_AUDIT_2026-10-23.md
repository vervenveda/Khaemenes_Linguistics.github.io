## Findings
1. **Present progression:** both courses have a Year I course navigator, foundation-to-application weekly structure and a four-unit portfolio. Sampled weeks align with an arc from script and greetings to practical and independent dialogue.
2. **Listening / speaking bottleneck:** no verified audio media detected in the ten sampled weeks of either course. Textual references to listening and oral assessment are not equivalent to independently usable listening recordings.
3. **Assessment quality:** the Week 16 and Week 32 pages provide four-skill 20-point domain frameworks, but secured unseen tasks and moderated keys are not in these public lesson pages. Weekly ten-item recognition quizzes do not prove productive ability.
4. **Source of language truth:** translator dictionary entries and editorial vocabulary drafts must not substitute for approved linguistic source materials. Latin transliteration must be marked approximate and not used as scored pronunciation evidence.
5. **Learner evidence:** Year I Week 32 portfolio gathers tasks for all four units and scores comprehensibility, vocabulary, grammar and independence. Add weekly portfolio capture and feedback criteria without saving personal work in public source.
6. **Accessibility:** ensure RTL Arabic, mixed-direction passages, Cyrillic print, speech availability, typed alternatives, teacher-reviewed audio and zoom/print across actual mobile browsers.
7. **Academic continuity:** Arabic standard vs regional dialect, Russian stress and cases need qualified review before student-grade signoff.
8. **Ecosystem resource drift:** Linguistics Hall now loads generated dictionary, errata, lexicon and transliteration before the core. The gateway still uses an older core and resources; separate compatibility pass required to keep protected homepage layout stable.
9. **Level calibration:** outline suggests proficiency-based placement; need a defined placement protocol and rubric, and clearly differentiate year-completion evidence from the 80% quiz readiness threshold.
