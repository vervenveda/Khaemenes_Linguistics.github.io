# Arabic I and Russian I — Year I Course-Wide Quality Assurance

Date: 2026-10-14 · Branch: main

## Verified fixes in this pass
- Both Week 32 final-framework pages now include a four-unit learning portfolio checklist with explicit 0–12 evidence rubric for each unit (48 points total).
- Portfolio evidence is formative and separate from the closed-book four-skill final examination.
- Each Unit 04 worked lesson already contains teacher-review and portfolio practice. Unit 02 also provides applied-practice tasks.

## Academic release blockers
1. **Content depth remains variable.** Newly added examples in Units 02–04 are useful, but the majority of pages still need deliberate reading passages, authentic short dialogue and integrated four-skill lesson design.
2. **Quizzes are formative only.** Some repeated generic study-strategy questions still consume question slots, and the end-to-end 80% grade interpretation has not been validated.
3. **Listening/speaking evidence is incomplete.** Qualified recordings or instructor-delivered assessments must be arranged.
4. **Assessments are public blueprints.** Week 16 and Week 32 still need secure unseen prompts, keys and second-reader moderation outside this public repository.
5. **Student journey unverified.** Full 64-page browser, keyboard, offline, print, multiple-profile persistence and recovery testing remain outstanding.
6. **Language-specialist signoff pending.** In particular Arabic inflection and dialect/register, Russian noun cases, adjective agreement, stress and idiomaticity require independent review.

## Practical evaluation gates
- A learner can navigate a full 32-week course and move between units without dead links.
- Every weekly question has one defensible answer and an explanation; prompts cover meaning, grammar and communication, not merely study habits.
- An assessor can evaluate portfolio evidence with a transparent rubric and record feedback privately.
- Each listening and speaking evaluation uses trusted human or independently reviewed recordings.
- All summative content is administered securely and remains unpublished.
- All end-user testing is performed in a real browser with keyboard and screen-reader checks and learner switching.

Status: development curriculum, not academically accredited or certified.
