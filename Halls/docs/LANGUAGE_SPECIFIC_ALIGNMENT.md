# LANGUAGE_SPECIFIC_ALIGNMENT.md

## Spanish
Primary language-specific benchmark: Instituto Cervantes Plan Curricular.
The Plan Curricular organizes objectives and inventories across A1–A2, B1–B2 and C1–C2 and includes grammar and communicative development.

## French
Primary language-specific benchmark: France Éducation international DELF / CEFR pathway.
The adolescent DELF junior/scolaire pathway includes A1, A2, B1 and B2. Khaemenes does not issue DELF diplomas.

## German
Primary language-specific benchmark: Goethe-Institut / CEFR.
Goethe exams span A1–C2 and test listening, reading, writing and speaking at the relevant levels. Khaemenes does not issue Goethe-Zertifikate.

## Mandarin Chinese
Primary language-specific benchmark: International Chinese Language Education Chinese Proficiency Grading Standards (GF0025-2021).
The official standard uses three stages and nine levels and was designed for teaching, learning, testing and evaluation. Khaemenes does not represent course completion as an official Chinese proficiency certificate.

## Japanese
Primary language-specific benchmark: Japan Foundation JF Standard.
JF Standard uses Can-do statements and emphasizes both task accomplishment and intercultural understanding. Its six Can-do levels use the same A1–C2 level labels as CEFR. Khaemenes does not issue Japan Foundation proficiency credentials.

## American Sign Language
ASL remains in the Atlas and in ACTFL World-Readiness scope. It is the recommended sixth full formal program.
