# Arabic I — Independent Grammar and Saudi Context Review Register
Date: 2026-10-23 · Source: Arabic I Weeks 01–32 · Status: independent review pending

This document is an **approval register, not an accuracy certificate**. The public lesson sequence is primarily Modern Standard Arabic (MSA). Saudi Arabic has multiple regional varieties; no single set of MSA examples should be labeled as universally Saudi colloquial speech. A qualified reviewer should distinguish MSA classroom models, Saudi usage and other regional usage explicitly.

## Review requirements
- **Writing system and vowels:** hamza position, connected letters, tanwin, optional versus obligatory diacritics, punctuation and Arabic-English bidirectional text.
- **Morphosyntax:** gender and number agreement, pronouns, construct state, case markers in vocalized MSA, tense/aspect, questions and prepositions.
- **Saudi dialect and register:** distinguish formal MSA from colloquial Saudi forms, indicate regional variation and appropriateness; do not replace MSA text with unsupported dialect spellings.
- **Naturalness:** native-speaker appropriateness, idiom, sociolinguistic politeness, transliteration limitations and cultural respect.
- **Listening approval:** audio provenance, dialect label, speaker permission, phonetic accuracy, accessible transcript, listening questions and reviewer sign-off.
- **Writing evidence:** authentic comprehension and original work across weeks, separate from the ten-question formative quiz.
