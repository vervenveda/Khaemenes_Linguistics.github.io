# PROGRAM_ARCHITECTURE.md

## Two-layer design

### World Language Atlas
The existing 206-dossier Atlas remains an open comparative archive of languages, scripts, families, signed languages, classical languages, heritage pathways and language-learning concepts.

### Five-Language Academy
Five languages receive complete formal ladders:
- Middle School 1–3
- High School I–IV
- College 101, 102, 201, 202

The old 40 modules are retained as a cross-language **Language Learning Foundations Library**, rather than being treated as Spanish, French, German, Mandarin or Japanese instruction.

## School-year courses
Middle-school and high-school courses are 36 weeks:
- six thematic units
- six weeks per unit
- Input & Modeling
- Vocabulary / Script
- Structure & Patterns
- Interpretive Lab
- Interpersonal Lab
- Presentational Performance

## College courses
College 101–202 are 16-week lower-division courses:
- four thematic units
- four weeks per unit
- Input + Structure
- Interpretive Evidence
- Interpersonal / Mediation
- Presentational Assessment

## AA-level boundary
“AA-level scope” means lower-division collegiate curriculum architecture. Khaemenes does not claim that completion automatically creates transferable college credit or satisfies a particular institution’s Associate of Arts degree requirement.
