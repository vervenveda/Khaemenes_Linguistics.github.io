# 2026-11-20 · French 101 instruction / assessment balance plan

## Audit finding

French 101 currently has a compact canonical spine of 18 lessons with strong mastery and cumulative assessment coverage.

Current assessment load includes:

- 18 lesson mastery quizzes at 20 questions each;
- Unit 01 cumulative knowledge assessment;
- Unit 02 cumulative knowledge assessment;
- cumulative midterm across Lessons 01–12;
- Unit 03 cumulative knowledge assessment;
- comprehensive final across Lessons 01–18.

The cumulative architecture is strong, but the next curriculum gain should come from **additional meaningful French encounters**, not additional objective testing.

## Protected rules

- keep 80% mastery;
- preserve earlier earned mastery and resumable assessment records;
- preserve newly drawn / parallel reassessment behavior;
- do not claim oral or listening proficiency from objective scores alone;
- keep independent speaking/listening observations separate from browser-scored knowledge evidence.

## Next curriculum revision priorities

1. Increase listening, reading, speaking and writing exposure between mastery events.
2. Add short spaced-retrieval language encounters before adding more questions.
3. Reuse earlier vocabulary and grammar in new communicative situations rather than repeating identical prompts.
4. Prefer short authentic/teacher-created dialogues, mini-readings, dictation, listening discrimination, guided speaking and written production.
5. Track delayed retrieval by lesson/domain when a future retention report is commissioned.
6. Keep cumulative midterm/final coverage, but review whether their length remains proportionate after the instructional spine expands.

## Boundary

This plan records curriculum direction only. It does not alter existing French 101 learner records, mastery thresholds, assessment answers, unit gates, midterm, final, or certificate authority.
