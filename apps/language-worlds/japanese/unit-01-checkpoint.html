Japanese · Unit 01 Checkpoint

After Foundation Lessons 01–04: a demanding five-domain application challenge. Knowledge is automatically scored; grammar, conversation, listening and performance require actual work and teacher review. No automatic course credit.

Part A · Knowledge (10 questions)

At least 8/10 demonstrates knowledge readiness, not full proficiency. Retry after feedback and additional study.

Part B · Grammar transfer

Create three original polite Japanese sentences: one destination with へ, one specific time with に, and one proposal using ましょう.

Part C · Original conversation

Write and perform a six-turn polite invitation with destination, time, acceptance or respectful refusal, and follow-up.

Part D · Listening comprehension

Practice dialogue (read aloud by an instructor, or device speech for ungraded rehearsal):

A：あした、としょかんへ いきますか。B：はい。ごご さんじに いきます。A：いっしょに べんきょう しましょう。B：いいですね。

Written transcript available; teacher supplies a different unseen prompt for assessment.

Listen to a teacher-read unfamiliar invitation twice without seeing the script. Identify destination, time and invitation; confirm meaning with the transcript afterward.

Part E · Teacher-reviewed performance

For each domain, score 0 = no evidence, 1 = emerging, 2 = developing, 3 = proficient, 4 = strong transfer. Evaluate correctness, independence and appropriate language use. A total 16/20 is a suggested readiness threshold, subject to genuine performance evidence.
