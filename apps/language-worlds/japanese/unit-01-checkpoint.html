Japanese · Unit 01 Checkpoint

After Foundation Lessons 01–04: a demanding five-domain application challenge. Knowledge is automatically scored; grammar, conversation, listening and performance require actual work and teacher review. No automatic course credit.

Part A · Knowledge (10 questions)

At least 8/10 demonstrates knowledge readiness, not full proficiency. Retry after feedback and additional study.

Part B · Grammar transfer

Create three original polite Japanese sentences: one destination with へ, one specific time with に, and one proposal using ましょう.

Part C · Original conversation

Write and perform a six-turn polite invitation with destination, time, acceptance or respectful refusal, and follow-up.

Part D · Listening comprehension

Practice dialogue (read aloud by an instructor, or device speech for ungraded rehearsal):

A：あした、としょかんへ いきますか。B：はい。ごご さんじに いきます。A：いっしょに べんきょう しましょう。B：いいですね。

Written transcript available; teacher supplies a different unseen prompt for assessment.

Listen to a teacher-read unfamiliar invitation twice without seeing the script. Identify destination, time and invitation; confirm meaning with the transcript afterward.

Unit 01 · Independent Transfer Challenges

Grammar-transfer challenge: Write five original polite Japanese statements for a park or community-event visit: destination marked by へ, specific time marked by に, invitation using ましょう or ませんか, affirmative or respectful negative answer, and a follow-up. Identify why へ and に were chosen, and maintain polite endings consistently.

Independent listening: An instructor prepares an unseen 5–7-turn polite Japanese invitation with a new destination, time and acceptance or refusal. Read twice without displaying text. Record (1) when, (2) where, (3) proposed activity, (4) response, and (5) one inference about the next action. Check particles, mora timing and politeness with a trusted speaker; the visible practice dialogue is not the exam prompt.

Language-specific rubric guidance

Japanese-specific performance anchors: Knowledge—understand place/time and polite invitations; Grammar—correct へ, に and ましょう/ませんか with new details; Conversation—six coherent original turns and polite acceptance/refusal; Listening—unseen invitation facts plus inference; Performance—intelligibility, politeness, appropriate pacing and self-correction. Scores 3–4 require sustained independent communication.

Readiness safeguards: Earn at least 8/10 on the knowledge quiz, at least 16/20 overall on the reviewed rubric, and at least 3/4 in each of grammar, conversation, listening and performance. Original work and reviewer feedback are required. This is a local educational checkpoint, not an external proficiency certificate.

Part E · Teacher-reviewed performance

For each domain, score 0 = no evidence, 1 = emerging, 2 = developing, 3 = proficient, 4 = strong transfer. Evaluate correctness, independence and appropriate language use. A total 16/20 is a suggested readiness threshold, subject to genuine performance evidence.

Japanese · Teacher review anchors

Use these criteria when evaluating the existing grammar, conversation and listening evidence. They clarify the existing reviewer rubric; they do not create a new grade or override stored assessment results.

Grammar and writing: Kana accuracy, particles, sentence order and appropriate politeness level.

Speaking and listening: Intelligible mora timing and context-appropriate formal/informal expression. Use new instructor-supplied listening prompts for evaluation; text-to-speech rehearsal does not prove listening mastery.

Reading and communication: Explain meaning in context, respond to an unfamiliar example, and document corrective feedback. An entered reviewer label is not authenticated certification.
