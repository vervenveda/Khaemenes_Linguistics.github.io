Unit 02 Applied Challenge

Independent communication assessment following Lessons 05–08. This challenge is not passed by quiz points. Teacher-reviewed evidence in grammar, original conversation, listening comprehension and overall communicative performance is required.

1. Grammar transfer

Write and explain a new sentence with に for time, で for location of action, を for object, and an appropriate 〜たい or 〜ませんか expression.

2. Original conversation

Compose a six-turn courteous exchange inviting someone to a meeting, asking for a time, changing the plan, and requesting an item. Do not copy a model from the earlier lessons.

3. Independent listening assessment

Teacher presents an unseen, verified Japanese conversation containing an invitation, location and schedule change. Student identifies three details.

Instructor must supply a new spoken passage, checked for accuracy, that is not provided as advance practice. Provide an accessible equivalent as appropriate.

4. Reflection and revision

Explain one error you corrected and how you made the conversation more natural or appropriate.

5. Human performance rubric · 20 points

Score 0–5 in each category: 0 absent, 1 limited, 2 emerging, 3 functional, 4 effective, 5 independent and accurate. Provisional Unit 02 achievement requires at least 16/20 with no domain below 3, plus specific evidence and human review. This is not externally certified proficiency.
