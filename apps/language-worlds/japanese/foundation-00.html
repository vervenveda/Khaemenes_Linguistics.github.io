Japanese: Writing System and Numbers

ひらがな・カタカナ・数字

Start with the 46 basic hiragana and 46 basic katakana, then numbers and short words. Kanji is introduced later; some everyday words combine scripts.

Study order: script recognition → numbers 0–10 → short words → greetings in Lesson 01. This is an ungraded readiness studio and does not replace teacher-reviewed assessment.

Hiragana • 46 basic characters

あいうえおかきくけこさしすせそたちつてとなにぬねのはひふへほまみむめもやゆよらりるれろわをん

Trace characters, identify similar forms, say sounds aloud, and explain how writing connects to pronunciation.

Katakana · 46 basic characters

アイウエオカキクケコサシスセソタチツテトナニヌネノハヒフヘホマミムメモヤユヨラリルレロワヲン

These are basic forms. Voiced sounds, combinations, and small kana come next.

Numbers · 0–10

0 — ゼロ1 — いち2 — に3 — さん4 — よん5 — ご6 — ろく7 — なな8 — はち9 — きゅう10 — じゅう

Challenge: read the numbers forward and backward, then identify three randomly chosen values without the model.

Word Reveal · Short words first

Reveal a word, attempt reading it before seeing its meaning, then use it in a new phrase. Begin with short written forms and progress gradually. For Chinese characters, length is counted in characters rather than Latin letters.

Ready?

Ready for greetings?

Polite greetings and self-introductions. Check letter or character recognition, read numbers 0–10, and explain a few short words first.
