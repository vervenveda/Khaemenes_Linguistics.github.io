Spanish — Lesson 03

Numbers, age, and questions

This lesson builds on the previous one. Complete the comprehension challenge, then create an original performance for teacher review.

1. Language model and transcript

A: ¿Cuántos años tienes? B: Tengo doce años. ¿Cuántos libros tienes? A: Tengo tres libros. B: Yo tengo dos.

Meaning: One speaker is twelve years old; they discuss having three and two books.

Text transcript remains available offline.

2. Grammar and transfer

Tengo means 'I have'. In Spanish, age is expressed with tener ('to have'): tengo doce años. Cuántos/cuántas agree with the noun.

Stretch challenge: Change the people, objects, places, or time to create a new situation; explain to a teacher why your grammar still works.

3. Comprehension and knowledge challenge

Five questions. Achieve at least 4/5 (80%) to demonstrate knowledge readiness. This is not full mastery without performance review.

4. Original performance

Create an original dialogue about age and belongings using two different numbers and at least one question.

For listening assessment, a parent/teacher should read the dialogue aloud or use verified audio, then ask comprehension questions without showing the transcript. Text-to-speech is demonstration only.

Teacher-reviewed performance challenge · Lesson 03

Knowledge questions are formative. Demonstrate grammar transfer, real comprehension, original production and communication. Each criterion earns 0–4 points; 13/16 is a provisional performance target, not automatic credit. Use fresh prompts to discourage memorized responses.
