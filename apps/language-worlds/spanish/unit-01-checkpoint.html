Spanish · Unit 01 Checkpoint

After Foundation Lessons 01–04: a demanding five-domain application challenge. Knowledge is automatically scored; grammar, conversation, listening and performance require actual work and teacher review. No automatic course credit.

Part A · Knowledge (10 questions)

At least 8/10 demonstrates knowledge readiness, not full proficiency. Retry after feedback and additional study.

Part B · Grammar transfer

Write three new Spanish sentences: one with voy a, one with porque, and one question. Explain your choices.

Part C · Original conversation

Write and perform a six-turn conversation between two people arranging a study meeting, using a question, destination, time, and reason.

Part D · Listening comprehension

Practice dialogue (read aloud by an instructor, or device speech for ungraded rehearsal):

A: Hola, ¿adónde vas mañana? B: Voy a la biblioteca a las tres porque necesito estudiar. A: ¿Quieres estudiar conmigo? B: Sí, nos vemos allí.

Written transcript available; teacher supplies a different unseen prompt for assessment.

Without reading during playback, listen to a teacher-read unseen variation twice. Note the destination, meeting time, reason, and what each speaker proposes.

Unit 01 · Independent Transfer Challenges

Grammar-transfer challenge: Write five original Spanish sentences in a new scenario (a museum visit rather than a library meeting): a destination using *ir a*, an accurate time expression, a question using *adónde* or *cuándo*, a reason using *porque*, and a negative response with a respectful alternative. Underline verb forms and explain two choices in English. Do not copy the model.

Independent listening: A parent or teacher prepares an unseen 5–7-turn Spanish conversation about a changed destination, meeting time, and reason. Read naturally twice, without showing the transcript. The learner records (1) destination, (2) time, (3) reason, (4) invitation or refusal, and (5) one inference about the speakers' plan. The reviewer records source and corrections. Device speech of the visible model is rehearsal only.

Language-specific rubric guidance

Spanish-specific performance anchors: Knowledge—explain vocabulary and questions in context; Grammar—accurate *ir a*, question formation and *porque* with independent substitutions; Conversation—six coherent turns with an appropriate invitation/refusal; Listening—four factual details and an inference from an unseen dialogue; Performance—intelligible pronunciation, natural turn-taking and self-correction. Scores 3–4 require independent use rather than copying.

Readiness safeguards: Earn at least 8/10 on the knowledge quiz, at least 16/20 overall on the reviewed rubric, and at least 3/4 in each of grammar, conversation, listening and performance. Original work and reviewer feedback are required. This is a local educational checkpoint, not an external proficiency certificate.

Part E · Teacher-reviewed performance

For each domain, score 0 = no evidence, 1 = emerging, 2 = developing, 3 = proficient, 4 = strong transfer. Evaluate correctness, independence and appropriate language use. A total 16/20 is a suggested readiness threshold, subject to genuine performance evidence.
