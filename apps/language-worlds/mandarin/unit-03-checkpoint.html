Unit 03 language-specific evidence review

Apply these anchors when using the existing Unit 03 teacher rubric. Do not replace its stored assessment evidence or scoring.

Grammar and writing: Require original production demonstrating word order, aspect markers, measure words, and accurate character choice.

Reading: Interpret an unfamiliar short passage and support an answer with textual evidence.

Listening and speaking: Evaluate tone accuracy, word segmentation, and comprehension of unfamiliar spoken Mandarin. Use an instructor-supplied unseen prompt rather than device speech rehearsal for formal review.

Review boundary: Feedback and submitted evidence support instruction; self-entered reviewer names are not authenticated, and practice alone does not confer course credit.
