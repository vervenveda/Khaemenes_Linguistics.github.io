Mandarin Chinese · Unit 01 Checkpoint

After Foundation Lessons 01–04: a demanding five-domain application challenge. Knowledge is automatically scored; grammar, conversation, listening and performance require actual work and teacher review. No automatic course credit.

Part A · Knowledge (10 questions)

At least 8/10 demonstrates knowledge readiness, not full proficiency. Retry after feedback and additional study.

Part B · Grammar transfer

Create three original sentences using time before the verb, 去 for movement, and 因为 for a reason. Explain the order in English.

Part C · Original conversation

Write and perform six short turns planning a meeting in Mandarin: ask where, state when, give a reason, and agree on a plan.

Part D · Listening comprehension

Practice dialogue (read aloud by an instructor, or device speech for ungraded rehearsal):

甲：明天下午你去哪儿？乙：我三点去图书馆，因为我要看书。甲：我们一起学习，好吗？乙：好，我们三点见。

Written transcript available; teacher supplies a different unseen prompt for assessment.

Listen to an unfamiliar, teacher-read short Mandarin dialogue twice; record the place, time and invitation. Check tone distinctions with qualified feedback.

Unit 01 · Independent Transfer Challenges

Grammar-transfer challenge: Create five new Mandarin sentences for a school-club meeting: include time before the verb, 去 + place, a question with 哪儿 or 几点, a reason using 因为, and an invitation with 一起…好吗. Provide characters and an English explanation of two word-order decisions. Avoid copying the library model.

Independent listening: A teacher or qualified speaker prepares an unseen 5–7-turn Mandarin exchange with different location, time, invitation and reason. Present twice without text. Record (1) day/time, (2) destination, (3) purpose, (4) response to the invitation, and (5) one inference. Review listening details and relevant tone distinctions against a verified source; visible synthetic speech is not the graded prompt.

Language-specific rubric guidance

Mandarin-specific performance anchors: Knowledge—meaningful recognition of characters, time and place; Grammar—correct time placement, 去, 因为 and question order; Conversation—six original linked turns and appropriate invitation; Listening—accurate key details and inference from unfamiliar speech; Performance—intelligibility including lexical tones, pacing, and self-repair. Scores 3–4 require productive use with feedback on tone accuracy.

Readiness safeguards: Earn at least 8/10 on the knowledge quiz, at least 16/20 overall on the reviewed rubric, and at least 3/4 in each of grammar, conversation, listening and performance. Original work and reviewer feedback are required. This is a local educational checkpoint, not an external proficiency certificate.

Part E · Teacher-reviewed performance

For each domain, score 0 = no evidence, 1 = emerging, 2 = developing, 3 = proficient, 4 = strong transfer. Evaluate correctness, independence and appropriate language use. A total 16/20 is a suggested readiness threshold, subject to genuine performance evidence.

Mandarin · Teacher review anchors

Use these criteria when evaluating the existing grammar, conversation and listening evidence. They clarify the existing reviewer rubric; they do not create a new grade or override stored assessment results.

Grammar and writing: Time-before-place ordering, measure words, and context-sensitive word order.

Speaking and listening: Tone production, tone recognition, and intelligible pronunciation. Use new instructor-supplied listening prompts for evaluation; text-to-speech rehearsal does not prove listening mastery.

Reading and communication: Explain meaning in context, respond to an unfamiliar example, and document corrective feedback. An entered reviewer label is not authenticated certification.
