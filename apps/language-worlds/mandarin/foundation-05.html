Khaemenes Academy · Unit 02 · Lesson 05

Mandarin Chinese · Plans and times

Knowledge, grammar transfer, independent communication, listening rehearsal and human-reviewed performance.

1 · Read and interpret

甲：你星期六想做什么？乙：我想去图书馆。甲：几点去？乙：上午十点。

Comprehension: Summarize what both speakers want, where the exchange takes place, and what changes.

2 · Grammar transfer

Use 想 + verb for intention; distinguish 星期六 (Saturday), 上午 (morning), and 十点 (ten o'clock).

3 · Independent conversation

Write a plan with an activity, day and time, then ask a follow-up. Do not merely copy the model. Include at least four meaningful turns; Lesson 08 requires six.

4 · Listening and inference

Text alternative / transcript

我星期六上午十点去图书馆。

Device speech is not a certified listening test. A teacher must provide a different, verified spoken passage for assessment.

5 · Knowledge rehearsal (5 questions)

Score 4/5 indicates knowledge readiness only; it cannot certify proficiency.

6 · Teacher performance review

Score 0–4 each for accuracy, grammar transfer, independent communication and comprehension. Maximum 16; 13+ suggests provisional performance readiness, with no domain below 2. A teacher must assess the student's original response and an independent listening prompt.
