Mandarin Chinese: Writing System and Numbers

拼音、汉字与数字

Mandarin has no alphabet. Begin with Pinyin initials, finals and tones, then study characters and numbers. Pinyin spelling is a pronunciation guide, not the Chinese writing system.

Study order: script recognition → numbers 0–10 → short words → greetings in Lesson 01. This is an ungraded readiness studio and does not replace teacher-reviewed assessment.

Word Reveal · Short words first

Reveal a word, attempt reading it before seeing its meaning, then use it in a new phrase. Begin with short written forms and progress gradually. For Chinese characters, length is counted in characters rather than Latin letters.
