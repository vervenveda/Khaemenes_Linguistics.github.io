Unit 03 · Applied Communication

Lessons 09–12 develop original plans, invitations, completed experiences, and clarification. Knowledge quizzes are formative. Independent listening and teacher-reviewed language performance are required for mastery.

  1. Lesson 09 · Travel planning

Unit 04 · Reasoning and Community Communication

Lessons 13–16 extend comparative language, explanation, completed experiences and cooperative planning. Each lesson stores independent student work separately, supports offline study, and keeps five-question practice formative. Teacher-reviewed proficiency remains distinct from quiz points.

  1. Lesson 13 · Comparing choices
  2. Lesson 14 · Giving reasons
  3. Lesson 15 · Recounting experiences
  4. Lesson 16 · Community project planning
  5. Unit 04 · Integrated teacher-reviewed performance checkpoint

Unit 05 · Reasoning, Information and Dialogue

Lessons 17–20 develop respectful persuasion, clear procedures, critical interpretation of information, and inclusive intercultural communication. Knowledge checks remain formative; independent listening and teacher-reviewed work are preserved separately for each learner.

  1. Lesson 17 · Persuasive communication
  2. Lesson 18 · Explaining procedures
  3. Lesson 19 · Interpreting information
