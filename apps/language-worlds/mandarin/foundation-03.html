Mandarin Chinese — Lesson 03

Numbers and basic possessions

This lesson builds on the previous one. Complete the comprehension challenge, then create an original performance for teacher review.

1. Language model and transcript

甲：你有几本书？乙：我有三本书。你有几支笔？甲：我有两支笔。乙：我们一起学习吧。

Meaning: B has three books; A has two pens; they propose studying together.

Text transcript remains available offline.

2. Grammar and transfer

有 means 'have'; 几 asks 'how many' for small quantities. 本 counts books; 支 counts pens. Numeral + measure word + noun is an essential pattern.

Stretch challenge: Change the people, objects, places, or time to create a new situation; explain to a teacher why your grammar still works.

3. Comprehension and knowledge challenge

Five questions. Achieve at least 4/5 (80%) to demonstrate knowledge readiness. This is not full mastery without performance review.

4. Original performance

Create a dialogue asking about books and pens with correct measure words and at least two different quantities.

For listening assessment, a parent/teacher should read the dialogue aloud or use verified audio, then ask comprehension questions without showing the transcript. Text-to-speech is demonstration only.
