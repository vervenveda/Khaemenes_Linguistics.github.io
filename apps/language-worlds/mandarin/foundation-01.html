Mandarin Chinese · Foundation Lesson 01

Greetings, names and tones

Learning goal: Greet a peer, ask their name, and distinguish tone information in pinyin.

Supplementary guided lesson. This practice does not replace the Hall's formal curriculum or establish course credit.

Read and understand

你好！我叫小明。你叫什么名字？— 我叫丽丽。很高兴认识你。

Hello! My name is Xiaoming. What is your name? — My name is Lili. Nice to meet you.

Speech synthesis is optional. The written passage remains available offline.

Study the language

  1. 你好 (nǐ hǎo) is a common greeting; Mandarin pinyin marks tone, not just spelling.
  2. 我叫 (wǒ jiào) introduces a name; 你叫什么名字？ asks what someone is called.
  3. Mandarin has four lexical tones plus a neutral tone in standard introductory descriptions.
  4. Characters and pinyin are complementary learning aids; pinyin is not a replacement for literacy.

Repeat the passage, then write a new, fictional introduction and question. Do not use personal identifying information for public assignments.

Five-question challenge

Complete all five questions. Four correct responses (80%) indicate a knowledge checkpoint only; authentic reading, writing, listening and speaking still require review.

1. Which characters commonly mean hello?
2. What does 我叫 introduce?
3. What does the mark in nǐ indicate?
4. Which characters are used to say thank you?
5. What does 你叫什么名字？ ask?
