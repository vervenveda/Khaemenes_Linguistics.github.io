Assessment foundations

This pilot follows the Academy's planned assessment blueprint: 20 questions per lesson (16 correct to pass), a 50-question unit exam (40 correct), and Monte Carlo question banks with at least one-third reserve. The midterm and final are planned after the later units. Formal assessments are not yet active; no simulated grades or certificates are issued from practice writing.
