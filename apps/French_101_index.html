Khaemenes Academy · French four-skill review guide

Use this independently of French 101 practice scores. A quiz pass or adjusted practice threshold does not demonstrate verified proficiency or award formal course credit.

  1. Reading: interpret an unfamiliar short French passage, explaining meaning and supporting details.
  2. Writing: write original French sentences using appropriate agreement, tense, accents and situational vocabulary.
  3. Listening: identify meaning from a new instructor-supplied audio clip; device-generated speech is rehearsal only.
  4. Speaking: sustain a brief comprehensible exchange with appropriate liaison, pronunciation and register.

For each skill, document the learner's independent work, an instructor's observations, a correction, and the next practice goal. Keep learner evidence private. Instructor identity and assessment quality must be verified outside this public page.
