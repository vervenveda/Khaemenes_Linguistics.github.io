## Progress authority

Legacy `noor_quran_advanced_v1` records are normalized into the `noorQuranDeepening.v2` model. A historical 70% pass flag does not survive unless its score independently reaches the current 80% threshold.

Because weekly checks contain six questions, weekly mastery requires at least 5/6. Final mastery requires at least 8/10.

## Privacy

Scores, dates, last open week, and certificate name remain in browser local storage. No information is transmitted. Export creates a local JSON record. Reset requires confirmation and removes current and legacy records from the device.

Any self-recorded recitation suggested by the curriculum is made outside this app. Learners should keep recordings private unless they deliberately choose to share them with a qualified teacher.
