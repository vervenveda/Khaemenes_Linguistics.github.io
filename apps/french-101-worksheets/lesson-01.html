French 101 · Unit 01 · Student Worksheet

Lesson 01 · Meet the French Alphabet

Directions: Complete all ten exercises. Write clearly and preserve accents. Show the reasons for your choices; use the ruled spaces to develop original answers. For independent retrieval tasks, close your lesson models first. This worksheet is formative practice and does not replace an official French 101 assessment.
Return to French 101

1. Write the 26 letters of the French alphabet in their correct order.

Use four groups: A–G, H–N, O–U, V–Z.

2. Complete the alphabet sequences: H, I, ___, K; P, Q, ___, S; V, ___, X.

Explain how you checked each missing letter.

3. Write the French letter names for A, B, C, D, E, F, and G.

Check your spelling against your lesson and correct errors.

4. Copy these accurately, preserving every mark: école; père; fête; Noël; garçon.

Circle each accented letter and label the mark where possible.

5. Explain the difference between the name of a letter and the sound it makes inside a French word.

Use école or garçon as one example.

6. Read: « Bonjour ! Je m'appelle Léa. Voici mon école. » Identify the greeting, the name and the word for school.

Explain what the short passage tells you.

7. Read: « Comment ça s'écrit ? » Write an appropriate reply that spells the name Léa.

Include the accent in the written spelling.

8. A student writes « Lea » for « Léa » and « garcon » for « garçon ». Correct both and explain the changes.

Describe the acute accent and cedilla.

9. Create a new four-line French exchange: greeting, introduction, spelling request and response.

Use a new name, not Léa or Hugo. Explain any spelling marks.

10. Close the alphabet model. Write A–Z from memory and identify two points you corrected afterward.

Record one question you would ask your teacher about French spelling.

Reflection and correction journal

Choose one mistake you made. Describe the original response, the correction, and how you will remember the distinction.
