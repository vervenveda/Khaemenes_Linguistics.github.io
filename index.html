Language Skill Studios

Each studio can grow into a focused app while remaining connected to one consistent Academy learning record.

“A language is not only learned. It is listened to, lived with, written through, and shared.”

Alphabet & Script Lab

Tracing, keyboard practice, character families, diacritics, and writing direction.

Pronunciation Studio

Sound comparison, IPA support, syllables, rhythm, tone, and listening drills.

Grammar Atelier

Sentence-building, patterns, conjugation, cases, word order, and clear explanations.

Conversation Commons

Role-play, everyday dialogues, polite forms, confidence practice, and speaking prompts.

Translation Workshop

Meaning, context, register, comparison, ethics, and human-centered translation practice.

Vocabulary Conservatory

Root systems, spaced review, thematic sets, word families, and personal dictionaries.

Reading Room

Graded readers, parallel texts, newspapers, poetry, classics, and comprehension tools.

Writing Desk

Journals, correspondence, composition, correction, prompts, and portfolio development.
